AULA Studio: installing
======================

1. Drag "AULA Studio" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Applications and double-click AULA Studio.
   macOS says it can't verify the developer, because this build isn't notarized by Apple yet.
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").

3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to "AULA Studio was blocked". Confirm with your password.
   You only need to do this once.

   Or, in Terminal:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AULA Studio.app"

4. When the app asks, allow:
   • Bluetooth: battery level and firmware version
   • Input Monitoring: mirrors your key presses on screen (nothing is recorded)

Requirements: macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
To change lighting, the screen or keyboard settings, connect the keyboard with its USB-C cable.

AULA Studio is an independent app. It isn't made by or affiliated with AULA or Epomaker.
